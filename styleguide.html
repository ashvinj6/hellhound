Mini Design System · v1.0

Style Guide

The visual language for the Hellhound on My Trail page. It borrows from Mississippi in 1937: shellac 78s, juke joints lit by lanterns, red clay roads, and paper gone yellow with age. Everything here lives in style.css as CSS custom properties and reusable classes.

01 · Color

Palette

Dark, warm neutrals do most of the work. Clay and ember are the only loud colors, so use them sparingly for emphasis, rules and interactive states. Brass is for labels and small details.

Neutrals

Night#14110F--night · page background
Shellac#1F1A17--shellac · cards, record
Smoke#2C2622--smoke · raised surfaces
Muted#8A7D6B--muted · footnotes, meta
Sepia#B9A27F--sepia · secondary text
Bone#E8DCC4--bone · body text, paper sections

Accents

Clay#A8432A--clay · rules, drop caps, record label
Ember#D9682F--ember · links, kickers, hover
Brass#C9A24A--brass · labels, subheads

02 · Typography

Typefaces
